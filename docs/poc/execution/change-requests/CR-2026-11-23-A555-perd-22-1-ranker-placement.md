Risk to watch: the same score computed in two languages can differ in the last binary digit, and the margin and floor are comparisons at a boundary. The parity vectors should therefore include cases at exactly the margin and exactly the floor, and the Architect should say whether scores are compared as scaled integers or exact decimals. This is a question, not a ruling.
