No shortcut may touch a Tier 0 gate or an invariant.").

Owner: EL; reviewed in the daily rhythm and on Fridays, and attached to the release evidence bundle (plan §5, §15, §13.4). A shortcut found without an entry is treated as a defect (REQ-OPS-004). Task OPS-02 opens and reviews the register.

| DEBT-01 | **Receipt authenticity is checked only in the Controller.** The `execution_rw` role may `INSERT` into `execution_receipt` (migration `0001`) and may change `decision_grant.status`; the guard of migration `0002` checks the *shape* of a receipt's signature (non-empty key, 64 lower-case hex characters), not the HMAC, because the database holds no key (PERD §41.1).
